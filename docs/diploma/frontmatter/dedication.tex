\chapter{Dedication and acknowledgements}
\begin{SingleSpace}

We dedicate this work to our families, who supported us through long nights of debugging and writing.

We thank our supervisor, Alua Myrzakerimova, for guiding the project from an initial prototype into a coherent end-to-end system, for her patience with iteration cycles, and for insisting on a level of operational discipline that ultimately defined the character of the work.

Finally, we acknowledge the open-source community whose contributions underpin every layer of the project: the maintainers of Django, Django REST Framework, Celery, OR-Tools CP-SAT, LightGBM, React Native, Expo, Terraform, and the many smaller libraries on which the system depends.
